Quot3 - local AI model notice (Milestone 22)

Quot3 can use a small text-embedding model that runs on this PC only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Model: multilingual-e5-small (intfloat/multilingual-e5-small; ONNX conversion Xenova/multilingual-e5-small)
Authors: Liang Wang, Nan Yang, Xiaolong Huang, Linjun Yang, Rangan Majumder, Furu Wei (Microsoft Research)
Licence: MIT (confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12; see docs/THIRD_PARTY_AI.md)
Licence text: https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-small (MIT)

The model files are not part of the source repository; they are fetched by tools/fetch-ai-model.mjs and packaged into the installer.
