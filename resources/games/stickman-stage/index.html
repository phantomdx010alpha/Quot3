Welcome to Stickman Stage

This is not a level-clearing game, but a stickman performance director's booth that belongs to you.

  • Number keys 1-6 switch six acts: disco, arm waves, moonwalk, weird run, flips and a closing bow
  • F hand flame throws fireballs to ignite the trash can at center stage for waste-to-power incineration
  • S summons a glowing sword that slashes light; T enters the golden super transform; effects can stack
  • Synthesized beats drive every move; switch moves on the beat and press Space to sync, igniting crowd hype
  • Turn on the auto director to sit back and watch a random show; one click also saves a neon stage screenshot
