Physics
Stones start from the left; power sets the distance and angle sets the sideways landing point. Getting close to existing stones causes explainable collision displacement.

Three stars
Reach the end target score after three throws using no more than the official reference total power; hinted demos give at most two stars. The model is only for abstract puzzle play and does not represent real ice or match strategy.

Twelve-End Line Puzzles

Four chapters add guard stones, double collisions and high-scoring button zones. The official enumerator walks three-throw sequences across 25 angle/power combinations, using the same collision and scoring function to find the lowest-total-power route to the target score.
