🧠 Strategy Tips

Black moves first and has the edge, which is why Renju gives Black forbidden moves. Attack with a "four-three": one move making a four and an open three at once — your opponent can only block one. On defense, stop open threes before they grow. Stay near the center early: the closer your stones, the more lines you threaten.

📖 About this game

Gomoku online on a standard 15×15 board: Black moves first, and the first to get five in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins. Play the computer at Beginner, Advanced or Master — the AI uses five-tuple evaluation with iterative-deepening alpha-beta search and both attacks and defends — or play a friend on the same screen. Optional Renju rules forbid Black's double-threes, double-fours and overlines, with forbidden points marked on the board. Play Black or White, undo, ask for hints, show move numbers, and enjoy stone-drop animations and a glowing five-in-a-row celebration.
