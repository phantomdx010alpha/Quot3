🧠 Memory Tips

Use the peek second wisely! Flip cards row by row in order, and turn positions into little stories — "the puppy in the top left is chasing the kitten in the bottom right" — they stick much better.

📖 About this game

Memory Match in your browser: flip two cards each turn; matching pictures make a pair, and finding every pair wins. Four difficulties — Easy 4×3, Normal 4×4, Hard 5×4 and Master 6×5 — four card themes (animals, fruits, space, ocean), timed solo play or 2-player turns, smooth 3D flips and a peek at the start. Great memory training for kids and families, completely free.
