🎮 Controls

  • 🖱️ Drag a gem onto a neighbor, or click one then its neighbor
  • 👆 Touch: press a gem and flick in any direction

💎 Special Gems

➖
4 in a line → Striped gem, clears a whole row or column
💥
L or T shape → Bomb gem, blasts a 3×3 area
🌈
5 in a line → Rainbow gem, swap it to clear every gem of that color
🎆
Swap two special gems together for a super combo!

🧠 Combo Tips

Match near the bottom: falling gems are more likely to cascade, and each cascade level raises the multiplier. When a 4 or 5 match is possible, hold off the plain three — special gems pay off much more!

📖 About this game

Match-3 jewel puzzle in your browser: swap two neighboring gems to line up three or more of the same color. Four in a row makes a striped gem, L/T shapes make a bomb, five in a row makes a rainbow gem, and special gems can be combined. 15 move-limited levels plus a 90-second blitz mode, with hints and auto-shuffle. Smooth on mouse and touch, completely free.
