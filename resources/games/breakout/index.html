🧠 Pro Tips

Sneak the ball above the wall and it will rattle around the top clearing tons of bricks! Grey steel bricks are unbreakable, red TNT bricks blast their neighbors, and chaining hits without touching the paddle stacks a combo multiplier.

📖 About this game

Breakout / Arkanoid in your browser: bounce the ball with your paddle and break every brick to clear the level. 10 handcrafted levels with normal, hard, steel, TNT and mystery bricks, plus 8 power-ups — expand, multi-ball, laser, fireball, catch, slow, extra life and shrink — and a combo multiplier. Mouse, keyboard and touch, completely free.
