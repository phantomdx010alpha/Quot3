Starry Composer

A pure WebAudio random melody generator and step sequencer. Pick a key, style and complexity, and a seed produces a reproducible 2- or 4-bar loop; edit melody, bass and drums cell by cell and mix the piece down to a WAV download.

  • Markov-style interval movement, with melody notes leaning toward the tonic
  • The pentatonic scale really uses the gong, shang, jue, zhi and yu intervals
  • Kick, hi-hat and snare are all synthesized in real time from oscillators and noise

Headphones recommended; after you start, the browser audio engine is activated.
